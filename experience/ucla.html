UCLA DCP — Lead Accessibility Engineer

2024 – Present

Overview

Since 2024, I’ve served as a Lead Accessibility Engineer with UCLA’s Disabilities & Computing Program (DCP) under the University Web Accessibility Initiative (UWAI). I ensure campus PDFs and digital documents are accessible and compliant with WCAG and Section 508, improving structure, readability, and screen-reader usability.

Key Contributions

Document Accessibility & Remediation

  • Remediated complex PDFs (reports, forms, slide decks) with correct heading hierarchy, reading order, lists, tables, and link semantics.
  • Authored clear, descriptive alt text for charts, multi-image figures, and data-dense visuals used in technical content.

Standards & Compliance

  • Applied WCAG 2.x and Section 508 requirements; validated tag trees, tab order, language metadata, and related checks.
  • Created concise checklists for headings, alt text, tables, and reading order to standardize remediation quality.

Consultation & Training

  • Delivered 1:1 consultations and small-group trainings for faculty/staff covering Acrobat workflows and accessible authoring practices.
  • Produced guides and training snippets for UWAI partners (e.g., grouping images, describing data visuals, exporting cleaner source PDFs).

Process & Impact

  • Established a repeatable workflow: auto-tag → heading audit → reading order → alt text → tables/lists → metadata/lang → final PAC checks.
  • Collaborated with departments and UWAI leadership to prioritize high-impact documents, improving accessibility scores across syllabi, policy docs, and training decks.
